# Research notes

When the user asks you to look something up or "research" a topic:

1. Fetch one or two primary URLs with `web_fetch` (docs, official pages, READMEs). Do not invent citations.
2. Write a short note into `workspace/research/<slug>.md` with:
   - the question
   - what you found (with URLs)
   - what is still unknown
3. Reply in chat with a few sentences and the path of the note you wrote.

If a fetch fails, say so and work from what you already know. Prefer official sources over random blogs.
